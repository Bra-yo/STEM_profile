About Shallom Sila

Empowering communities through education, leadership, and innovation

Shallom Sila

Shallom Sila is a globally award-winning educator, STEM advocate, Education Technology (EdTech) Consultant, and leadership mentor dedicated to expanding access to transformative education. His work equips girls and young people in rural and low-income communities with the skills, confidence, and leadership capacity needed to thrive in a rapidly evolving world.

Through robotics, AI literacy, digital education, and evidence-based learning approaches, he integrates innovation with purpose-driven leadership development. He is passionate about closing opportunity gaps and prepare future-ready leaders grounded in creativity, critical thinking, and strong values.

The Initiative

Founded to address inequities in access to quality STEM education, this initiative delivers structured programs in robotics, coding, artificial intelligence, digital learning innovation, and mentorship, designed to create sustainable and measurable impact.

Our Core Values

Excellence in Education

We are committed to providing high-quality educational experiences that inspire curiosity, foster critical thinking, and prepare students for future success, with a focus on excellence in STEM education.

Integrity and Character

We believe that true leadership is built on a foundation of integrity, honesty, and strong moral character. These values guide everything we do.

Community Impact

We are dedicated to creating positive, lasting change in communities through collaborative partnerships and sustainable development initiatives.

Innovation and Creativity

We encourage creative thinking and innovative problem-solving, empowering students to become pioneers in their chosen fields.

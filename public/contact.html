Frequently Asked Questions

How can I enroll my child in your programs?

You can enroll your child by filling out our online application form or visiting our office during business hours. We also host information sessions where you can learn more about our programs and meet our team.

Do you offer scholarships or financial aid?

Yes, we offer scholarships and financial aid based on need and merit. Please contact our admissions office for more information about available opportunities and application requirements.

How can I volunteer with Shallom Sila?

We welcome volunteers! You can apply through our website or contact our volunteer coordinator. We have various opportunities including mentoring, teaching assistance, event support, and administrative help.

What age groups do you work with?

We work with students from ages 8-18, with different programs tailored to specific age groups. Our programs are designed to be age-appropriate and progressive, building skills as students grow.

Do you partner with schools?

Yes, we actively partner with schools to bring our programs directly to students. If you're a school administrator interested in partnership, please contact our partnerships team.

How can businesses partner with you?

Businesses can partner through sponsorships, internship programs, mentorship opportunities, and resource sharing. Contact our partnerships team to discuss how we can collaborate.

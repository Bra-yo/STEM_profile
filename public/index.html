About Shallom Sila

Shallom Sila is a globally award-winning educator, STEM Equity advocate, Education Technology (EdTech) Consultant, and leadership mentor dedicated to expanding access to transformative education. His work equips learners, especially girls and young people in rural and low-income communities, with skills, confidence, and leadership capacity needed to thrive in a rapidly evolving world.

Through robotics, AI literacy, digital education, and evidence-based learning approaches, he integrates innovation with purpose-driven leadership development. He is passionate about closing opportunity gaps and prepare future-ready leaders grounded in creativity, critical thinking, and strong values.

Our programs are designed to bridge the gap between education and real-world application, providing students with the skills, confidence, and networks they need to succeed in an increasingly competitive global landscape.

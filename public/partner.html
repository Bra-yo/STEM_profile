Building Opportunity Together

Expanding access to transformative STEM education requires collaboration. We work with schools, educators, community leaders, organizations, and partners who share a commitment to advancing opportunities for girls and young people in rural and low-income communities.

Together, we can bridge educational gaps and prepare future-ready leaders equipped with technical skills, confidence, and ethical leadership capacity.

Why Partner With Shallom Sila?

Partnering with Shallom Sila means investing in the future of our communities and the world. Together, we can create transformative change that lasts for generations.

Our partners become part of a growing network of organizations committed to educational excellence, innovation, and social impact. Through collaboration, we amplify our collective impact and create opportunities that none of us could achieve alone.

Make measurable social impact
Access to talented future workforce
Enhance corporate social responsibility
Build brand reputation and trust

Who We Partner With

Schools & Educational Institutions

We collaborate with schools to implement robotics programs, coding workshops, AI literacy training, digital learning integration, and STEM pathway development aligned with CBC/CBE frameworks.

NGOs & Community Organizations

We partner with organizations serving rural and underserved communities to expand access to STEM education and leadership programs.

Corporate & Technology Partners

We welcome collaboration with technology companies and corporate partners interested in supporting digital inclusion, STEM equity, and future workforce development initiatives.

Educators & Trainers

We provide professional development, ICT integration support, and innovation-focused capacity building for teachers and education leaders.
